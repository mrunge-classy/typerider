How it works

No account, no e-mail, no cookie banner. Just a name and your fingers.

1

Pick a name

Type one or take one of the three suggestions. It's the only thing we store.

2

Choose a story

Der Nachtzug, Kaffee um sieben or The Lighthouse Keeper — six or seven lines each.

3

Type, then Enter

The clock starts on your first keystroke. Enter sends the line and pulls up the next.

4

Take your rank

WPM counts five characters as one word. Accuracy is measured per character.
